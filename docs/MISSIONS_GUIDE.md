# Mission & Kill Frenzy Guide

## Overview

Missions in GTA 2: City of Chaos are initiated via ringing payphones located throughout the city districts, or by discovering secret **Kill Frenzy** skull pickups hidden in dark alleys and rooftops.

---

## 📞 Payphone Missions

### 1. Zaibatsu Corporation Missions
- 🟢 **Mission 1: Cop Shakedown** (Green Phone)
  - *Objective*: Steal a marked Police Cruiser and deliver it to the Zaibatsu chop shop without sustaining critical vehicle damage.
  - *Reward*: $10,000, +25 Zaibatsu Respect, -10 Loonies Respect.
- 🟡 **Mission 2: Loonie Lab Purge** (Yellow Phone)
  - *Objective*: Infiltrate the Loonie chemical lab, eliminate the armed guards, and detonate the chemical vats using timed explosives.
  - *Reward*: $25,000, +40 Zaibatsu Respect, -25 Loonies Respect, Micro-SMG ammo.
- 🔴 **Mission 3: Executive Extraction** (Red Phone)
  - *Objective*: Escort Chairman Trey Fabrik's armored Z-Type through heavy Yakuza ambush blockades to the harbor helipad.
  - *Reward*: $60,000, +60 Zaibatsu Respect, -30 Yakuza Respect, Electro-Gun unlock.

### 2. The Loonies Missions
- 🟢 **Mission 1: Joyride Pandemonium** (Green Phone)
  - *Objective*: Hijack an Ambulance, install a car bomb at the bomb shop, and park it in the middle of a Zaibatsu checkpoint before detonation.
  - *Reward*: $12,000, +25 Loonies Respect, -15 Zaibatsu Respect.
- 🟡 **Mission 2: Fire and Fury** (Yellow Phone)
  - *Objective*: Armed with a Flamethrower, burn down 4 Zaibatsu billboard antennas across the Downtown expressway within 180 seconds.
  - *Reward*: $30,000, +45 Loonies Respect, -20 Zaibatsu Respect.
- 🔴 **Mission 3: Asylum Breakout** (Red Phone)
  - *Objective*: Ram the gates of the high-security prison with a heavy SWAT Van and rescue Elmo's top lieutenants while under 4-Star police heat.
  - *Reward*: $75,000, +70 Loonies Respect, Rocket Launcher.

### 3. The Yakuza Missions
- 🟢 **Mission 1: Dockside Honor** (Green Phone)
  - *Objective*: Eliminate 5 undercover Zaibatsu informants at the cargo docks using only melee weapons or silenced pistols.
  - *Reward*: $15,000, +30 Yakuza Respect, -15 Zaibatsu Respect.
- 🟡 **Mission 2: Grand Casino Heist** (Yellow Phone)
  - *Objective*: Intercept an armored security truck, blow open the rear vault doors with grenades, and deliver the loot bags to J-Town.
  - *Reward*: $40,000, +50 Yakuza Respect, -25 Zaibatsu Respect.
- 🔴 **Mission 3: Dragon's Wrath** (Red Phone)
  - *Objective*: Destroy the Zaibatsu penthouse suite using RPGs and eliminate the reinforcements sent by the FBI.
  - *Reward*: $100,000, +80 Yakuza Respect, Infinite Dual Uzis.

---

## 💀 Kill Frenzies (Rampages)

Hidden skull tokens trigger 120-second frenzies with unlimited ammunition:
1. **Rocket Havoc**: Destroy 15 vehicles in 120 seconds using the RPG.
2. **Flamethrower Inferno**: Torch 30 enemy syndicate members in 90 seconds.
3. **Drive-By Terror**: Score 20 drive-by kills while speeding in a sports coupe.
4. **Tank Rampage**: Crush 25 vehicles using the National Guard Tank.
