# Gangs & Faction Lore of Anywhere City

## 1. The Zaibatsu Corporation (Z-Corp)
- **Symbol**: Red "Z"
- **Headquarters**: Downtown High-Rise Financial District
- **Leader**: Trey Fabrik
- **Gang Car**: *Z-Type* (Sleek black limousine with red neon trim and twin turbochargers)
- **Weapon of Choice**: Dual Silenced Pistols, Micro-SMG, High-Tech Electro-Gun
- **Ideology**: Total corporate dominance, cybernetic enhancements, and pharmaceutical distribution.
- **Rivals**: The Loonies (view them as chaotic pests), The Yakuza (competing for tech contracts).

---

## 2. The Loonies
- **Symbol**: Yellow Happy Face with manic grimace
- **Headquarters**: Sunnyside Mental Asylum & Industrial Slums
- **Leader**: Elmo
- **Gang Car**: *Dementia* (Acid-green customized buggy with reinforced roll cage and megaphone)
- **Weapon of Choice**: Molotov Cocktails, Flamethrowers, Car-Bombs
- **Ideology**: Pure anarchy, psychoactive liberation, and dismantling all civil order.
- **Rivals**: The Zaibatsu Corporation (hate corporate structure), The Rednecks (territorial clashes).

---

## 3. The Yakuza
- **Symbol**: Blue Dragon Kanji
- **Headquarters**: Waterfront Harbors, Shipping Docks, and J-Town
- **Leader**: Johnny Zoo
- **Gang Car**: *Miara* (High-performance sports coupe in metallic cobalt blue)
- **Weapon of Choice**: Katanas, Dual Uzis, Combat Shotguns
- **Ideology**: Honor among syndicates, luxury car trafficking, and dockside smuggling.
- **Rivals**: The Zaibatsu Corporation (industrial espionage), The Russian Mafia.
